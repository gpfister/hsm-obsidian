## Executive Summary

This session executed Phase 2 (Specify) of the development lifecycle for two critical HSM capabilities:
1. **Interactive Reset Confirmation & Complete Factory Reset Zeroization**: Implementing safe interactive prompts and `-y/--yes` bypass flags in `hsmutil reset`, accompanied by full firmware zeroization across all 32 CryptoCell CC310 hardware key slots, wiping the Shamir Secret Sharing (SSS) master secret, committing cleared defaults to NVS, and transitioning the hardware state to `HSM_STATE_UNCONFIGURED` (breathing BLUE LED).
2. **Multi-Device Auto-Discovery & Disambiguation**: Eliminating silent arbitrary serial port selection in multi-dongle environments by defining `ErrMultipleDevicesDetected`, mandating `--port` or `--serial` targeting, supporting dual serial/port targeting in `hsmutil key migrate`, and preserving non-blocking device enumeration in `hsmutil devices`.

The authored specification artifact was presented to the user via interactive approval gate and formally accepted.
